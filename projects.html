Capstan Driven Robotic Manipulator (I)

Capstan drives are a special type of reducer offering low inertia, high stiffness, and high precision. This project examines the design and utility of capstan reducers in robotic applications and demonstrates their use in a 5-bar manipulator.

Skills: Arduino, 3D Printing, MATLAB, SolidWorks, Wiring & Soldering

Cartesian Variable Stiffness Mechanism (I)

In industrial manufacturing, robots excel at pick-and-place tasks and handling large heavy loads, however, these applications also cause exacerbated wear due to the high inertial loads. To mitigate this, we introduce the design of a novel cartesian variable stiffness mechanism placed between the robot arm and its end effector aimed to reduce these forces and provide extended capabilities. In this work, we developed several concepts for tuning stiffness and present a working prototype with a demonstrated use case in a pick-place scenario.

Skills: Abaqus FEA, Arduino, CNC Mill, 3D Printing, Fusion 360 CAM, Manual Lathe, MATLAB, SolidWorks, Wiring

Remote Ultrasound Robot (G)

Under-resourced remote areas have limited access to skilled diagnostic ultrasound examinations. However, to accurately diagnose the most common birth defect CHD (Congenital Heart Disease) and other heart complications, technical expertise is often required. To address this we sought to create a teleoperable robot capable of replicating the high dexterity motions necessary for pediatric diagnostic ultrasound in remote communities.

Skills: Arduino, SolidWorks, MATLAB, Wiring

Other Past Projects

Rubik's Cube Solver (I)

The Rubik's Cube is an iconic three-dimensional puzzle invented in 1974 but became well-known within pop culture during the 1980s. While the inventor Erno Rubik intended its use to teach students about building 3D objects, he quickly discovered its difficulty, taking him nearly a month to solve it. After learning how to solve a Rubik's Cube and seeing others build automated mechanisms capable of completing the various algorithms, this project set out to build a similar machine. While the project was not fully completed, it provided several valuable lessons outlined below and remains a candidate for future continuation.

Skills: Arduino, SolidWorks

Lunar Anchoring Device (G)

The NASA Micro-g NExT program offers challenges for undergraduate students to design and manufacture a product to be tested by NASA. Our challenge was to fabricate a mechanism that can ultimately anchor to the surface of the moon while being lightweight, easy to handle, and other requirements.

Skills: SolidWorks

5-DOF Arduino Robot (I)

After recently getting introduced to Arduino, I set out to build an operable robot from scratch. For this project, I looked to expand my knowledge of programming, CAD, and 3D printing and learn new skills like soldering, wiring, and how to estimate the necessary joint torques depending on the robot's payload.

Skills: Arduino, 3D Printing, SolidWorks, Soldering & Wiring
