A little about me

I am a mechanical engineer recently graduated from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, specializing in robotics, mechatronics, and mechanisms. My thesis research focused on a novel variable stiffness mechanism for industrial robotics, resulting in publications at ASME IDETC conferences and a pending U.S. patent.

I also led the design and project team for a Remote Ultrasound Robot aimed at expanding access to specialized healthcare in remote communities. During my internship at Relativity Space, I designed a modular gearbox from scratch, performed both hand calculations and FEA, and validated the design through testing to inform future improvements.
